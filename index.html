Mitigating Visual Degradation in MLLMs via Spatial-Spectral Visual Anchor Learning

SSVAL

At a glance

Visual representations should not fade as reasoning gets deeper.

TL;DR: SSVAL learns persistent visual anchor prompts from vision foundation models, preserving perceptual evidence throughout MLLM inference while adding only 0.04% FLOPs.

The problem

Strong vision enters the model. Too little survives.

Visual instruction tuning can cause internal MLLM representations to rapidly deviate from their original semantic states.

Direct alignment with external vision foundation models enriches visual semantics, but does not stop representations from drifting across deeper LLM layers. SSVAL separates knowledge absorption from inference efficiency: Visual Anchor Prompt Injection learns stable anchors from external VFMs, while spatial and spectral alignment losses provide complementary intermediate-layer supervision. The VFM and auxiliary objectives are removed after training, leaving a lightweight inference path.

Method

Learn from rich visual priors. Anchor them at inference.

01

VAPI

Prompts absorb complementary VFM knowledge through gated cross-attention, then persist as visual anchors during inference.

02

SpaRA

Multi-scale spatial alignment preserves both fine-grained visual detail and global structural information.

03

SpeRA

Frequency-domain alignment reinforces global semantics and complementary high-frequency cues at intermediate layers.

Results

Consistent gains, negligible inference cost.

+6.47%CV-Bench 2D
+7.86%MMVP
+1.55%inference parameters
+0.04%inference FLOPs
